Realtor License Checker

Enter a full name and state to jump straight to the right official license-verification search.

There's no single national database of real estate licenses — each state runs its own, and sites like Google or realtor.com can't be auto-scraped for a verified answer. This gives you one-click searches: the state's official license records and ARELLO are the authoritative check; realtor.com and Zillow just show marketing profiles, which can exist even with a lapsed license or belong to someone else with the same name — treat those as a lead, not confirmation.
